# Deliberately no "Disallow: /admin". The admin pages carry a noindex meta tag
# (see src/routes/admin/_layout.tsx), and a Disallow would stop Google fetching
# them at all - which stops it ever reading that tag. Google then indexes the
# bare URL as "No information is available for this page", which is worse than
# the noindex it would otherwise honour. The two mechanisms conflict; noindex
# is the one that actually keeps a page out of the index.
